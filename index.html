Music Store Sales Analysis

A digital music store keeps its data in 11 linked tables. I queried them in PostgreSQL to find out where the money comes from, who the customers are, and what sells, then visualized the results in Tableau.

What I found

  • The USA has the most invoices (131) and the most customers (13), followed by Canada (76 invoices, 8 customers).
  • Rock earns $2,609 of the $4,709 total, so the catalog depends heavily on one genre.
  • Queen ($190) and Jimi Hendrix ($185) are the top-earning artists.
  • Yearly revenue stays between $1,139 and $1,222, so the store needs a growth plan beyond its current customers.

The SQL behind it

Two of the queries I wrote against the PostgreSQL database.

-- Which countries have the most invoices?
SELECT COUNT(*) AS invoice_count, billing_country
FROM invoice
GROUP BY billing_country
ORDER BY invoice_count DESC;
-- Which genres earn the most revenue?
SELECT g.name AS genre,
       ROUND(SUM(il.unit_price * il.quantity)::numeric, 2) AS revenue
FROM invoice_line il
JOIN track t ON il.track_id = t.track_id
JOIN genre g ON t.genre_id = g.genre_id
GROUP BY g.name
ORDER BY revenue DESC;

How I built it

  • Restored the PostgreSQL backup and explored the 11 tables.
  • Wrote SQL queries to answer business questions, using joins and aggregation.
  • Cleaned the data (padded text fields, end-of-file markers) and checked that revenue totals matched the invoices.
  • Built charts in Tableau and combined them into one dashboard.
